Training rules

Warm up 5–10 minutes, plus lighter practice sets for your first major lift.

Most working sets: stop with 1–2 good reps left. Rest 2–3 minutes on compound lifts, 60–90 seconds on smaller lifts.

Once you complete all sets at the top of the rep range with good technique, increase the weight by the smallest available increment.

Weeks 1–2: Build into the programme if needed.
Weeks 3–10: Full prescribed sets.
Weeks 11–12: Review progress and reduce volume if fatigued.

Nutrition: approximately 140 g protein daily. Your initial calorie estimate is 2,300–2,500 kcal, adjusted by progress.

The exercise images are visual references; use your gym machine instructions for setup and safety.
